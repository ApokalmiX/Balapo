# Balapo — Future Development Ideas

## Purpose

This document is a starting point for discussing the future of Balapo. Ideas listed here are proposals to explore, not commitments or an implementation roadmap.

## Current Direction

- Balapo adds custom Jokers, with an emphasis on Endless Mode runs.
- Two Joker packs can currently be enabled independently in the mod configuration.
- A third, optional development pack is enabled by default and reserved for experimental Jokers. See [Development Joker Pack](DEVELOPMENT_PACK.md).
- The mod uses Lua, Love2D, and Steamodded.

## Questions to Guide Development

- What should make Balapo recognizable compared with other content mods?
- Should future content focus on Endless Mode, the first eight antes, or both?
- How much complexity should a single Joker introduce?
- Should each pack have a clear theme or support a broad range of strategies?
- Which existing mechanics deserve more support before adding new ones?

## Areas to Explore

| Area | Possible direction | Question to resolve |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New Jokers | Support less explored strategies and create interesting combinations. | Which play styles need more options? |
| Pack identity | Give future packs a coherent theme or gameplay purpose. | What should distinguish each pack? |
| Endless progression | Explore scaling effects, long-term goals, and meaningful trade-offs. | How can progression stay interesting without making choices trivial? |
| Balance | Review costs, rarity, activation conditions, and scaling of existing content. | Which effects feel too weak, too dominant, or too situational in play? |
| Other content | Consider vouchers, decks, or challenges that complement the Jokers. | Does each addition strengthen the mod's identity? |
| Presentation | Improve artwork, effect descriptions, and consistency between cards. | Can players understand an effect and its consequences quickly? |
| Configuration | Consider optional content groups or settings when they serve a clear purpose. | Which options would players actually use? |
| Compatibility | Review interactions with base-game mechanics and relevant mods. | Which combinations should receive explicit playtesting? |
| Documentation | Keep installation information and content descriptions aligned with releases. | What information is missing for a new player? |

## Idea Template

### Idea: [Working title]

- **Concept:** What changes or gets added?
- **Player benefit:** What interesting choice or experience does it create?
- **Gameplay rules:** What triggers the effect, and what are its limits?
- **Interactions:** Which existing cards or mechanics could combine with it?
- **Balance questions:** What could make it too strong, too weak, or exploitable?
- **Presentation needs:** Artwork, descriptions, and feedback required.
- **Technical questions:** Implementation uncertainties and compatibility concerns.
- **Playtest criteria:** What observations would justify keeping or changing it?
- **Priority:** Undecided / Low / Medium / High.
- **Status:** To discuss.
- **Decision and rationale:** To be filled after discussion or playtesting.

## Choosing What to Try Next

For each candidate, consider:

1. Fit with the intended identity of Balapo.
2. Variety and quality of player decisions.
3. Interactions with existing content.
4. Clarity of the effect and its description.
5. Implementation, artwork, and maintenance effort.
6. Balance risks and ease of playtesting.

Prefer a small, playable prototype when an idea's value depends on how it feels in a run.
